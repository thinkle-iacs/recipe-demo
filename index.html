Tollhouse Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

Step 1: Wet Ingredients

Cream the butter and sugars (brown and white).

Then add the 2 eggs and vanilla and mix.

Step 2: Dry Ingredients

Next, add flour. Lazily sprinkle baking soda and salt into flour, and give it a quick stir with the spoon so you don't get any clumps.

Add in chocolate chips.

To Form Cookies

Plop 1-2 Tbsp. size cookies onto a sheet, in a 3 × 4 pattern.

Bake!

Bake at 350°F for 8-10 minutes.

Take out when they still look a little bit under-baked. If they are not sufficiently crinkly, give the pan a whack on the counter to create some ripples.

Leave on the pan for 1-3 minutes to finish carryover cooking, unless you overbaked them in which case get them off in a flash.
